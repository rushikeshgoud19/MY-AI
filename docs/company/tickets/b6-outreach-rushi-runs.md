# The outreach motion Rushi will actually run

`wayfinder:grilling` (HITL) · OPEN · blocked by: B3, B5

## Question

Claude drafts, Rushi sends. That is fixed and not up for redesign. What is
undecided is the motion around it — and it has to be one Rushi is still running in
three weeks, not one that looks good on day one.

- **Which channel?** Direct email, a marketplace listing where buyers come to you,
  posting build logs publicly, or existing communities. A marketplace needs no
  outreach at all and takes a cut — that trade is a real option here, not a
  cop-out, especially for someone with limited hours.
- **Volume per day Rushi can sustain.** Ten researched messages beat a hundred
  generic ones, and a hundred needs infrastructure and risks the sending domain.
- **What Claude prepares:** per-prospect research, a drafted message, and the
  specific reason this prospect has this problem. Rushi reviews and sends.
- **How a reply is handled.** Claude does not talk to a customer directly — it
  drafts, Rushi sends. Decide the turnaround, because a warm lead goes cold while
  waiting for a human to be awake.

## Hard constraints

No scraped lists, no fake identities, no volume that constitutes spam, no pretending
a message was written by a person who did not read it. Beyond the ethics: it burns
the sending domain and the accounts, and there is no recovering a young business
from that reputation. If the numbers only work at spam volume, the offer is wrong —
go back to the offer.
