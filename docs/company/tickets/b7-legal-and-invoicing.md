# What is legally required before invoicing a stranger

`wayfinder:research` · **MOSTLY COLLAPSED 2026-08-05** — the marketplace absorbs it

## Question

Small, boring, and much cheaper to answer before a customer than after one.

- Can an individual in India invoice for services without registering a business,
  and up to what point?
- GST registration threshold for services, and whether export of services changes
  it.
- Anything specific to being a student — does it affect eligibility, tax treatment,
  or any scholarship or institutional condition that applies to him.
- What an invoice must legally contain.
- Whether a written scope of work is needed for the arrangement to be enforceable,
  and what a minimal one looks like.

## Resolution — 2026-08-05: selling through a marketplace absorbs most of this

Choosing Fiverr (B6) removes the parts of this ticket that assumed a direct client
relationship. What is left is short.

**Answered already, in [B1](b1-can-rushi-get-paid.md):**

- Individual can invoice without registering — sole proprietorship exists by default.
- GST threshold for services is ₹20 lakh; Telangana is not special-category.
- Invoice contents without a GSTIN.

**Dissolved by the channel:**

- **No invoice to write.** Fiverr bills the buyer and reports earnings to the seller.
  The invoice format from B1 applies only to direct clients, which this is not.
- **No scope-of-work contract to draft.** The gig description plus Fiverr's terms
  are the agreement. The enforceability question in the original ticket assumed a
  bilateral contract that will not exist for order #1.

**Genuinely still open:**

| Item | Note |
|---|---|
| Money arriving through banking channels | **Satisfied.** Payoneer or bank transfer are authorised channels. This is precisely the distinction that made Clustly a FEMA problem and makes Fiverr fine |
| FIRA for export of services | **Open.** Payoneer supplies documentation but does not auto-issue FIRA; Skydo and Winvesta do (B1). Matters at volume, not for order #1 |
| Declaring foreign income on the ITR | **Open, Rushi's, at filing time** |
| Student-specific conditions — any scholarship or institutional rule restricting earning | **Unknown. Only he can check this**, and it was not researched because it depends on his specific institution |

**Ruled out of scope:** drafting terms of service, a privacy policy, or a client
contract. All three would be motion — the marketplace supplies them, and producing
them before a first customer is exactly the kind of tidy-looking work the map warns
against.
