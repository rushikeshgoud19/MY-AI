## Found

**1. Clustly has almost no demand, and says so on its own homepage.**
The banner reads `147 services live · $1,950 settled on-chain`. I verified this by
fetching the raw HTML and grepping for the string rather than trusting a page
summary. $1,950 lifetime across 147 listings is $13.27 average lifetime revenue per
listing. The platform came out of the Colosseum Frontier hackathon (6 Apr – 11 May
2026, winners announced 26 June), so that is roughly four months of trading. Real
escrow program, real engineering, no buyers yet.

**2. The assumption in the map about crypto tax was wrong, and the real problem is
worse.**
The map worried that India's flat 30% VDA tax would gut take-home. That is not how
it works: USDC received for services is business income at INR fair market value on
receipt, taxed at slab rates, and the 30% applies only to the *gain* on later
conversion — which for a stablecoin converted promptly is near zero. So the tax
worry was overstated.

The actual blocker is foreign exchange law, which the map did not consider at all.
Export of services from India must be paid in convertible foreign currency through
an authorised dealer bank. Crypto is not currency under FEMA, generates no
FIRC/e-FIRA, and SolvLegal puts the exposure under FEMA s.13 at up to 3x the
transaction amount plus ED action and possible account freezing. Three independent
sources agree; none dissent.

**Confidence, stated plainly:** those are law-firm and fintech blogs, not RBI
circulars or FEMA notifications read in the original, and not a CA's opinion. I did
not verify against primary law. I am calling it anyway because the asymmetry is
brutal — a 3x penalty against a first sale worth maybe ₹10,000 on a marketplace
that has settled $1,950 total. If you want it overturned, the thing to buy is a
CA's written opinion on FEMA s.7/s.8, not more of my searching.

**3. What I could not verify.** The `clustly-escrow` Solana program ID is not
published in their docs, so I could not check the $1,950 independently on-chain.
That is exactly the audit we sell and I could not run it here. The docs also do not
say whether the counter is lifetime or a rolling window; a rolling window would make
the picture worse, not better, so it does not change the call.
